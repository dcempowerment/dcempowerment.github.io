About DCE Digital Studio

A more refined online presence starts with intentional design.

I'm Miata Moses, the designer and strategist behind DCE Digital Studio. With an Associate's degree in Computer Science Information Technology and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science with concentrations in Programming, Cybersecurity, and Information Technology, I bring both technical expertise and strategic thinking into every project.

I am currently pursuing my Master's degree in computer science, continuing to strengthen the advanced knowledge that supports the structure, security, and performance behind my digital work.

I design more than just websites. I create secure, high-performing digital experiences that help businesses look established, operate efficiently, and grow with confidence.

My work is for brands that want to feel more polished online, not just visible, but credible, refined, and positioned at a higher level. Every design is intentional, combining luxury aesthetics with a real technical foundation.

A beautiful site is important, but a premium website should also be secure, fast, strategically built, and aligned with the level of business you are building.
