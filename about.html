Meet the Founder

I’m Miata Moses, the designer and strategist behind DCE Digital Studio.

I created DCE Digital Studio to help small businesses, service providers, entrepreneurs, and growing brands build a more refined online presence. My work blends premium visual design with practical technology support so business owners can look more established and operate with more confidence.

With an Associate’s degree in Computer Science Information Technology and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science with concentrations in Programming, Cybersecurity, and Information Technology, I bring both creative direction and technical understanding into every project.

I am also pursuing my Master’s degree in Computer Science, continuing to strengthen the knowledge that supports the structure, security, and performance behind my digital work.

My approach is simple: your website should not just exist online. It should communicate value, create trust, support your business goals, and reflect the level of professionalism you want people to associate with your brand.
